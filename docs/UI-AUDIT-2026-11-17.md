# UI audit and build spec (17 Nov 2026)

Console at http://127.0.0.1:8700/app, branch `claude/ui-revamp-4fd0ec`. I clicked through all 15 routes at 1440px in light and dark mode and at 375px in light mode, then ran a scripted WCAG contrast and overflow pass.
The owner asked for a full investigation and build, so the build goes ahead on this spec without a separate sign-off.

## Findings

### Lineage (the owner wants a "wow factor")
- **River view (`components/lineage/River.tsx`).**
  - The view is static and cramped. Bars are tiny, most of the canvas is empty, and the click targets are small.
  - Nothing animates, and there is no focus mode, minimap or hover card.
- **SVG labels have no contrast.** Labels drawn inside bars ("B", "!", "now") measure 1.0:1 in both themes.
- **Insights sit in the wrong place.** The insight cards sit above the river instead of beside the selection.
- **Faint controls.** The disabled "Step ›" control and the "Live" / "Back to live" controls are faint.

### Contrast (both themes)
- **Breadcrumb separator.** `span.sep "/"` measures 1.7–1.8:1 on every page.
- **Chain pills on Work detail.** `.stage-current` and `.stage-tone-running` (`styles.css` around lines 617 and 622) put muted detail text, such as "working for build · fleet-gate", on the accent background. It is unreadable in both themes.
- **Faint disabled buttons** on Now, Budget, Roles, Policies and Evals.

### Mobile (375px)
- **Touch targets under 24px.** Now has 93, Work has 100, Settings has 9, and Lineage, Trace, Plan and Policies have 4 each.
- **Tables collapse into cards that read poorly.**
  - Sub-text lands in the label column, for example "macOS · IP" and "runner update available" on Nodes.
  - An orphaned "Running" appears on Work.
  - Titles are truncated.
- **Overflow and layout:**
  - The chain pill overflows its card.
  - Filter selects have ragged widths.
  - A Budget button overflows.
  - Models repeats the model name and has long rows for each node.
  - The node link's underline collides with its status dot.
- **Palette** shows keyboard hints on touch devices.

### Function gaps (from use cases I made up)
- **"Spawn a Sonnet review of task X" can't be done from the Spawn dialog.**
  - The dialog has no kind or model field.
  - The prompt sits below the plumbing fields.
  - The estimate says "unknown tokens".
  - The full-mode checkbox is separated from its label.
  - The close control is the text "Close" rather than an X.
- **Work detail has rough edges.**
  - The attempt ID renders as a button inside the table.
  - Tokens are shown as prose instead of a stat.
  - "Trace" is a lone link.
- **These work well:** palette search, the Budget layout and Work detail overall.

## Inspiration
- **React Flow:** animated SVG edges with particles. It also shows the performance caveat of throttling animation when the graph is large.
- **Dagster:** asset-graph facets, plus collapsible groups to keep big graphs readable.
- **Temporal:** compact, timeline and full views of the same run.
- **LangSmith:** trajectory and trace trees.
- **Paradime:** lineage focus and highlighting of the upstream and downstream path.
- **Dagger:** live pipeline visualisation.

## Build A: lineage rebuild (hand-written SVG, no new dependencies, per SPEC-ui-revamp)
1. **Animated flow.** Edges are bezier paths with moving particles (SVG `animateMotion`). Particles run only on running edges; done edges are static and failed edges are red. A live attempt pulses. Above 150 edges, animate only the selected path. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
2. **Pan, zoom and drag.** Wheel or pinch zooms, dragging pans the background, and nodes can be dragged to a new position (kept in local state). A "Fit" button and a minimap with a viewport rectangle are included.
3. **Click and focus.** Clicking a node opens a side panel with model, account, kind, stage, tokens, gate, verdict, duration and links to Work and Trace. While a node is focused, nodes outside its upstream and downstream chain dim to 25%. Esc clears the focus.
4. **Hover card.** It shows title, state, model, elapsed time and tokens.
5. **Insight.**
   - **Header strip:** critical-path length, number of retries, gate pass rate, token total and the slowest stage.
   - **Badges:** failed stages and reroutes get an icon badge with a legend.
   - **Group toggle:** a "Group by work item" toggle collapses and expands the groups.
6. **Readable at any size.**
   - Bars are at least 28px tall.
   - Labels sit outside the bar when it is narrow.
   - Contrast is at least 4.5:1 in both themes.
   - The view stays usable at 375px: a touch pan works, and the side panel becomes a bottom sheet.
7. **Tests.** Vitest covers the layout and focus-set logic in `lib/lineageGraph.ts`: upstream and downstream sets, the critical path and the zoom clamp.

## Build B: polish
1. **Contrast fixes.** Fix the breadcrumb separator, chain pills, disabled controls and SVG labels so each reaches 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for UI, in both themes.
2. **Touch targets.** Every target must be at least 24px at 375px; aim for 44px on primary actions.
3. **Mobile cards.** Each field's sub-text stays with its value, titles wrap to 2 lines, filters lay out in an even grid, Models drops the duplicated name and collapses the per-node rows, and the node link's dot no longer collides with its underline.
4. **Spawn dialog.**
   - The prompt field goes first.
   - Add kind and model selects, with "auto" as the default.
   - Show a token and cost estimate based on the kind.
   - Fix the checkbox label.
   - Use an X close button.
5. **Work detail.** Show tokens as a stat, render attempt IDs as monospace links, and turn the Trace link into a button.
6. **Palette.** Hide the keyboard hints on touch devices (`pointer: coarse`).
7. **Gate.** Vitest and `tsc` must pass. Rebuild `ui/dist` and commit it with `git add -f`.
